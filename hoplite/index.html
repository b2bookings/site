Conversations

Definitions. A dial is any outbound call placed on the Hoplite call sequences. A conversation is a call where the rep spoke with a person at the account. A qualified conversation is one where the rep delivered the full pitch and the prospect responded, positively or negatively; a referral counts only when a specific contact is named. New calls are added automatically every two hours during the business day.
